Social Autopost Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who we are

Social Autopost is a personal application operated by one individual (the "owner") on the owner's own computer. It is used only with the owner's own social media accounts. Contact: eloratemplatestudio@gmail.com.

What data the app handles

When the owner connects an account (for example TikTok), the app receives a login token from that platform. The token lets the app send the owner's videos to the owner's own account. The app also reads the basic profile name of the connected account to show that the connection works.

Where data is stored

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. Login tokens are kept in a private file on that computer. We do not run a server, we do not collect data on a server, and we do not sell, share or disclose data to anyone.

How data is used

Data is used only to upload the owner's own videos at the times the owner scheduled. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. The owner can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app under Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and services, or by deleting the app's settings folder. No copy is held elsewhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
